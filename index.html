    Questions before you sign.

    What happens to Acrobat during the pilot?

    Nothing changes for anyone outside the pilot group. Pilot users keep their existing tools installed, so there is no risk to day-to-day work while we measure results.

    How much time does the pilot take from our team?

    Lena's team spends a few hours on deployment and the IT/Technical Validation Survey. Pilot users simply use Nitro for their normal work and answer a short survey at the end. Our Solutions Engineer runs everything else.

    What if the pilot doesn't meet our success criteria?

    Then you don't buy, and you'll know exactly why. The criteria are written into the Pilot Mutual Activity Plan, and the Executive Briefing reports against each one.

    How do you handle our security review?

    We share our security and compliance documentation with your InfoSec team at the start of the pilot so the review runs in parallel, not after.

    Can we deploy Nitro silently across all clinics?

    Yes. We validate your deployment model (NLS or DRM-free), SSO and data residency during the pilot, using the same installers you will use for production.

    What do you need from us to get started on rollout?

    A signed order form, your AP contact, and a Nitro admin contact. From there our Customer Success team schedules onboarding within a week.

    Questions we usually get.

    Who is our day-to-day contact during the pilot?

    Priya, your Solutions Engineer. She runs the pilot with Lena's team, joins the weekly check-ins and owns the usage and survey reporting.

    What happens at the Executive Briefing?

    We present your pilot results to Dana and Mark: survey results, usage, productivity, sustainability, and the time and dollars saved, projected across all 2,400 seats. It's the decision meeting.

    Who supports us after we sign?

    Marcus, your Customer Success Manager, leads onboarding, admin training and end-user webinars, and runs a 30-day adoption review after go-live.

    Can we pilot Nitro Sign APIs too?
